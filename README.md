# shipped-without-engineers

A methodology paper for delegating work to AI, written by a non technical product manager, proven on one real build.

This is not a product launch. The deliverable here is the method. Discovery interview, written sign off, a checkable definition of done, cheap models doing the labor, an expensive model doing the judgment, cross model quality review, and acceptance only against the definition. To prove the method is real and not just opinion, one small real need of the author's was built this way from start to finish, with every handoff saved unedited.

## What is in here

- `paper/`, the whitepaper. The method, the lessons, and reusable templates, written for anyone who delegates work to AI. Not written for engineers.
- `tasks/`, the evidence. One folder per delivered task from the worked example, containing the discovery interview, the signed PRD, the engineering brief, the cross model code review, and the acceptance verdict, exactly as they happened, including rejections and rework.
- `SCOREBOARD.md`, the numbers. Questions asked, first pass acceptance, defects caught in review, cost per task.
- `SPEC.md`, an early artifact. The first product idea from discovery, later rejected at PRD sign off in favor of the methodology framing. Kept unedited because the rejection is part of the evidence.

## The one line thesis

AI teammates are only as reliable as the definition of done they are given, and writing that definition is a product management skill. AI delegation needs product managers.
