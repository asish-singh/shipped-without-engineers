# Task 01, discovery interview

Held 2026-07-12 between Asish (PM) and Claude (engineering lead). Answers are verbatim, unedited. Asish opened by choosing the worked example and setting constraints before question 1.

## The PM's framing of the need

> maybe a tool that works in the background of my computer and measures what time i am spending doing what during my day job , and is non obstrusive, and helps track things so i can review at the end of the week and month how i can optimize my work schedule and time

## Constraints added before question 1

> yes. so the tool actually needs to be shipped and running on my mac every day and track what i do from 6pm ist to 4.45am ist on weekdays (remember i do night shifts so i am working on saturday morning till 4.45am but not on monday morning)
>
> everyday it should track and as per that the project in github needs to update with the findings but not expose my real work activity. it needs to be an ongoing project. maybe the application can collect data and i can go to github and maybe click a button RUN and it merges insights into the repo tracker or somethhing

## Question 1. What is the single decision the review must help you make?

> where i am most productive, how to make out more time to be more productive and cut the nonsense

## Question 2. How should the tool tell productive from waste? What does productive look like on your screen?

> productive is actively working on any browser, chatting on slack, or meetings which generate transcripts which are productive and get work done for me and not waste my time

## Question 3. Name the nonsense. What are your actual time sinks?

> fake meetings based on transcript quality
> slack huddles without any actionable roadmap

## Question 4. Where do your meetings and transcripts live?

> google meet, google calendar, gmail
> i would go to calendar and pull up transcripts for meetings i participated in

## Question 5. What should the Friday review be, and what makes the monthly review worth it?

> both
> and a monthly review would be, stop doing xyz, or stop interacting with this person so much they are a time waster, or write and research in a particular manner instead of what you did for something,etc

## Interviewer's note for the record

The headline discovery. The PM's nonsense is not entertainment or distraction, it is work shaped waste, meetings with empty transcripts and huddles that end with no owned next step. The product is therefore a work quality tracker, not a screen time tracker. Second discovery, the weekly deliverable is a decision memo, not a dashboard, charts support the memo rather than replace it.
